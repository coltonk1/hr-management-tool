# HR Management Tool (CodeHub - Best Semester Project)

The HR Management Tool was a full-featured HR platform concept built by a team of four during a CodeHub semester project. The project aimed to handle employee management, payroll, and general HR workflows in one integrated system. It ultimately won **Best Semester Project** at CodeHub.

---

## Project Overview

* **Purpose**: Provide an all-in-one HR management system, covering employee records, payroll, and administrative tools.
* **Stack**: Java backend, with a web frontend designed for ease of use by HR staff.
* **Team Size**: 4 developers (collaborative team-based effort).
* **Outcome**: Recognized as **Best Semester Project** by CodeHub.

---

## Development Process

1. **Planning**: Scoped to cover core HR features: employee onboarding, record-keeping, and payroll functionality.
2. **Backend Development**: Built in Java, focusing on reliability, modularity, and future scalability.
3. **Frontend & UI**: Designed interfaces for HR managers to add, update, and track employees using Next.js.
4. **Team Collaboration**: Split responsibilities across backend, frontend, and integration, coordinating through regular team meetings.

---

## Challenges

* **Ambitious Scope**: For a student project, a full HR/payroll suite was very ambitious. Building features like payroll calculations and secure employee data management was challenging, and we were unable to complete these features with other responsibilities taking the majority of our time.
* **Complexity of HR Systems**: Real HR tools require legal compliance and secure handling of sensitive data. As a student project, the team could only approximate this.
* **Coordination**: Coordinating between four developers with different experience levels required strong communication and task division, we struggled to work together efficiently at the beginning.

---

## Key Achievements

* **Award Recognition**: Won **Best Semester Project** at CodeHub.
* **Teamwork**: Demonstrated mostly effective task division, collaboration, and integration.
* **Technical Growth**: Provided early experience in backend design with Java, authentication, and database integration.

---

## Lessons Learned

* Large-scale systems require careful scoping to stay realistic.
* Handling sensitive data like employee and payroll info highlights the importance of security and compliance.
* Team communication and division of labor is as critical as technical skill in delivering complex projects.
* Ambitious projects, even if not production-ready, can be valuable learning milestones.

---

## Current Status

The HR Management Tool is no longer maintained, but it remains a significant milestone. It was the team’s first exposure to building a production-scale idea in a collaborative setting.
